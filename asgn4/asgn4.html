Grader Info

The sphere is the only thing with light toggle. The voxels have lambertian lighting with a small ambient light.

The lights default to being inside of the sphere and teapot, to fix this just move them out with the sliders. You can also just check the move light button to have the light orbit around about 10 units away.

The flashlight is the spotlight implementation. Press both enable flashlight and enable flashlight update. This will place the spotlight at the camera position pointing in the look direction. To stop moving the spotlight uncheck the flashlight update and the spotlight will stay in the last place you were.

The teapot replaces the sphere. I felt that it made more sense since it is kind of just a test. I didn't update all the shaders with the full blinn-phong lighting because I thought it would both look kind of bad. So while the normal visualization works, with the voxels the point lights and spot light don't.

The lighting is integrated with the voxels a little. The voxels have a directional lambertian light. I skipped the specular because I feel the voxels look better matte, it gives it a plastic almost lego look.
